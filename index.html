Before you collect.

Answers to a few questions about choosing and bringing a work home.

Ask the studio
What is the difference between an original and a print?

An original is the one-of-one painting itself. A print is a matte stretched-canvas reproduction, signed and numbered by the artist.

Do originals include authentication?

Yes. Every original arrives signed with a studio certificate of authenticity.

What size should I choose?

Use the true-scale wall previews on each product page to see how the artwork will look in your space. You can also explore our print size guide.

How are works shipped?

All artwork is carefully packed. Originals travel insured with tracking, with complimentary shipping across India. Canvas prints are corner-protected and securely boxed.

Can I see more detail before I buy?

Of course. Contact the studio to request close-ups, scale images, or a short studio video of any available work.
