Frequently asked

Before you collect.

Do originals include authentication?

Yes. Every original arrives signed with a studio certificate of authenticity.

How are works shipped?

All artwork is carefully packed. Originals travel insured with tracking; canvas prints are corner-protected and securely boxed.

Can I see more detail first?

Of course. Ask for close-ups, scale images, or a short studio video of any available work.

What is the difference between an original and a print?

An original is the one-of-one painting itself. A print is a matte stretched-canvas reproduction, signed and numbered by the artist. Compare them

What size should I choose?

Every work can be seen drawn to scale on a wall. Open the size guide
